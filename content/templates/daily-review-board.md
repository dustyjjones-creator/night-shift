## Approval Check

- [ ] Source finding is sanitized and suitable for public discussion.
- [ ] Draft contains no credentials, internal identifiers, or sensitive lab data.
- [ ] Technical claims are accurate and appropriately scoped.
- [ ] A human explicitly selected the final status.

Approval moves a reviewed copy to the approved archive; it does not authorize
or perform publication.
